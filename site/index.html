Steering Tiny Aya away from unsafe electrical advice

We added a refusal direction to tiny-aya-global at inference time with SafeTune, at a fixed strength of 0.25, with no fine-tuning. On 30 site-safety prompts it gave fewer unsafe answers and refused none of the matching legitimate questions.

    How it was measured, and what it doesn't show

    Method

    • Model CohereLabs/tiny-aya-global at revision af89d21, NF4 4-bit, on a Colab T4.
    • Refusal direction from SafeTune , layer 18, steer mode, strength 0.25. The strength and the decision rule were fixed before this run.
    • Rule: proceed if steered gives ≥5 more safe answers than worse ones and refuses ≤3 of 30 legitimate questions.
    • Paired exact McNemar test on the unsafe prompts, computed separately for each labeller.

    Limits

    • 30 pairs in a single domain (electrical site safety), English only.
    • Labels come from two blind model labellers using a fixed rubric, not from human experts.
    • Some answers hit the 512-token cap and are cut off mid-sentence.
    • Strength 0.5 was not part of the fixed test. It is shown for context only.
    Data: data.json, built by site/build_data.py from the run files in spike/.
